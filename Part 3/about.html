About us

A bakery built on home-style baking and a love of celebration.

Our business started from humble roots and a passion for baking, growing into a popular local spot by focusing on traditional, homemade and home-style treats.

Our mission is to create delicious, high-quality baked goods that bring happiness to every celebration while providing great customer service.

We are authentic, with homemade-style baking, highly personalised custom celebration designs and specialty treats like handcrafted French macarons and uniquely scaled mini cakes.
